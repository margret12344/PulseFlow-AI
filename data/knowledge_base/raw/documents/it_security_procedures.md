# IT Security Procedures

## Purpose

This synthetic document describes general IT security
reporting and initial response procedures.

## Common Security Concerns

- Suspicious login activity.
- Phishing emails.
- Unexpected account lockouts.
- Suspected malware.
- Unauthorized access attempts.
- Lost or stolen IT equipment.

## Suspicious Email Reporting

1. Do not click suspicious links or attachments.
2. Do not reply to suspicious messages.
3. Record the sender and message details.
4. Report the email through the approved reporting channel.
5. Escalate suspected phishing incidents to the IT Security Team.

## Suspected Account Compromise

1. Record the reported symptoms.
2. Identify the affected account.
3. Avoid requesting passwords or authentication codes.
4. Escalate immediately to the IT Security Team.
5. Follow authorized account protection procedures.

## Security Incident Escalation

Escalate immediately when:

- Unauthorized access is suspected.
- Sensitive information may have been exposed.
- Malware activity is reported.
- A device containing organizational data is lost.
- Multiple accounts show suspicious activity.

## Required Ticket Information

- Incident description.
- Affected system or account.
- Time detected.
- Relevant error messages.
- Known affected users or devices.
- Actions already taken.

## Security Restrictions

Do not investigate beyond assigned authorization.
Do not delete potential incident evidence.
Do not share sensitive information through unsecured channels.
Do not disable endpoint protection or other security controls.

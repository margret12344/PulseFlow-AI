# Hardware Troubleshooting

## Purpose

This synthetic document describes initial troubleshooting
procedures for common computer hardware problems.

## Common Symptoms

- Computer does not power on.
- Monitor displays no signal.
- Keyboard or mouse is not responding.
- Printer is not working.
- Device overheats or shuts down unexpectedly.

## Initial Troubleshooting

1. Identify the affected device and its asset number.
2. Check power and cable connections.
3. Verify that the monitor and peripherals are connected.
4. Record visible warning indicators or error messages.
5. Determine whether the problem affects other devices.
6. Avoid opening equipment or performing unauthorized repairs.

## Printer Troubleshooting

1. Check printer power and network connections.
2. Verify that the printer displays an error message.
3. Check whether the correct printer is selected.
4. Record paper or toner warnings.
5. Escalate persistent hardware faults to the Hardware Support Team.

## Escalation Conditions

- Device cannot power on.
- Hardware damage is visible.
- Overheating or unusual burning smells are reported.
- Multiple devices experience the same hardware issue.
- Initial troubleshooting does not resolve the problem.

## Required Ticket Information

- Device type and asset number.
- Device location.
- Description of the issue.
- Error indicators.
- Time the issue started.
- Troubleshooting steps already attempted.

## Safety Notes

Do not open electrical equipment without authorization.
Disconnect damaged equipment from use when safe to do so.
Follow approved equipment handling procedures.
